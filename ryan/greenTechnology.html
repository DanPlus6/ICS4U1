Green Technology

What is Edge Computing?

Green technology is designed to protect the environment by reducing environmental disruptors like pollution, excess harvesting of natural resources, and greenhouse gas emissions. It uses sustainable solutions that are environmentally efficient to help address climate change. This technology aims to balance technological development with environmental protection and long-term sustainability.

Benefits of Green Technology

Green technology can benefit Indigenous communities in Canada by improving access to sustainable energy, which in turn can benefit the environment and habitability of those communities.
