Collaborative Research Areas - Agriculture

How does this collaborative research area use Computer Science?

The agriculture area uses computer science through the usage of machine learning models, data analytics, cloud computing, wireless networks, computer vision and embedded systems to collect, analyze and advance agricultural techniques based on agricultural data. Used together, these technologies can help to detect crop disease, classify weeds, estimate biomass, predict frost, monitor crop health, and optimize irrigation and water management.

How does this collaborative research area use Computer Science?

Some positive impacts of this collaborative research area include improving food security, crop health, reducing production costs, optimizing resource usage, mitigating climate change, and improving farmer quality of life.

Some negative impacts however may include concerns regarding privacy and security due to large data collection for machine learning, rural employment displacement, limited accessibility to advanced technology for small-scale farms, and over-reliance on technology leading to loss of traditional agricultural skills.
