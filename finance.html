Collaborative Research Areas - Finance

How does this collaborative research area use Computer Science?

In the research areas of accounting and finance, computer science is seen in the usage of artificial intelligence for solving various finance problems, including classification, clustering and regression.

For classification, a machine learning model can be trained and used to solve binary classification problems (e.g. analyzing trends in customer credit history and other business data to accept or reject loan applications) or multiclass problems (e.g. analyzing investors' business history and data for classifying sentiment and confidence).

As for clustering problems, a model can be trained to use various clustering techniques such as partition clustering, density-based clustering, and hierarchical clustering to solve various clustering problems. This may include clustering brand strategies, assessment of social impact, detecting corporate fraud, optimizing portfolios, segmenting stock customers, and evaluating performance.

Finally, for regression, a model can be trained to predict continuous random variables and forecast future events based on current data. Examples of regression problems include predicting stock price trends by examining market signals, predicting corporate performance based on key performance indicators, or forecasting cryptocurrency prices based on global events.

How does this collaborative research area use Computer Science?

The major benefit of artificial intelligence in the finance industry and research area is the optimization of manual work. For the longest time, classification, clustering, and regression problems had to be solved by hand, leading to high expenses and time consumption, but with the implementation of automated artificial intelligence systems, these financial problems can be solved much quicker.
