Industrial Internet of Things

What are Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT)?

Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) is the use of smart sensors, devices and software to collect and analyze information in industrial and manufacturing environments. IIoTs are used in areas where reliability and safety are critical.

Effect on Economy

IIoTs allows for manufacturers to monitor data, such as machine parameters, settings, adjustments, temperature, and humidity, which can then be used to detect patterns. It can improve the performance of processes on the factory floor, fix problems more efficiently, and improve manufacturing performance, which can make production plants more efficient, increasing the economy.
