Emerging Technologies

DNA-based Data Storage

What is DNA-based Data Storage?

DNA-based data storage is the process of storing binary data from synthesized DNA strands. In the digital era where the software industry experiences massive growth, there is great potential for DNA-based data storage for information technologies because of DNA’s high storage density. However, this technology is currently limited by high costs and slow data reading/writing speeds.

What is DNA-based data storage's impacts on a developing society?

Such relatively new technology that is still developing would have low accessibility due to high costs, espeically for developing societies.

Howerver in the long run, DNA-based data storage can provide a highly space-efficient and long-lasting method for preservation of digital information, such as important government documents, scientific research, medical data, and cultural heritage; it can optimize energy and physical infrastructure requirements of large data centers, reducing environmental impact.
