What is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that generates content such as images, text, music, or code. You may have probably heard of many examples of Gen AI, which include ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Codex, Sora, Grok, and more.

What are Gen AI's impacts on a minority in Canadian society?

As powerful and useful as Gen AI may be, it requires massive datacenters to run; the AI boom has led builders to eye indigenous lands for expansion of hyperscale datacenters, which is blatant exploitation of indigenous land, just like with water, coal, and oil.
