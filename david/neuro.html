Neuromorphic Computing

What is Neuromorphic Computing?

Neuromorphic computing involves replicating or simulating the brain (of animals, humans, or even a new creation) to create powerful computing systems.

As an example for neuromorphic computing, you may have heard of the complete scan and simulation of a fly's brain, which has allowed the creation of simulations of a fly's behavior and even allows a fly to play chess (if you're wondering, it has ~400 elo and it's better than me 😩).

What are Neuromorphic Computing's impacts on the world economy?

Research and development of Neuromorphic computing can lead to great expansion of the neuroscience and related biology areas and industries, and of course continue the boom of the software industry in this digital age. Though the global neuromorphic computing market is projected to nearly quadruple by 2030, at least by value, the rise of the quantum age at similar time poses uncertainty to the focus of the tech world and market's hype.


Though AI technology and its industry has continuously grown, the technology's improvement is approaching a wall.

Current (Gen) AI is fundamentally limited by mathematical & computer algorithms it is built on; AI can find trends in data and replicate what exists in excess, but cannot truly create new things as it cannot think or have consciousness, which is one of the main reasons so many companies have invested into AI. pass this wall and even achieve AGI (human-level intelligence), AI has to be fundamentally changed and Neuromorphic Computing is the solution.
