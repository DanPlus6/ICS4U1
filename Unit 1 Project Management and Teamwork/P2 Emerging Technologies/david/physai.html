Physical AI

What is Physical AI?

Physical AI are systems where artificial intelligence interacts with the physical world, beyond the digital world. Physical AI systems usually include sensors, motors, and other components that allow the system to interact with the real world. Examples include robotic arms and automated machinery in factories, drones, humanoid robots, and more.

What is Physical AI's impact on most of Canadian society?

Physical AI could transform Canadian society by increasing productivity, improving workplace safety, and addressing labour shortages. However, it may also replace various jobs, displacing workers (especially those of less education), as well as increase concerns regarding privacy, inequality, and safety.

Honestly, just imagine hundreds of thousands of factory workers losing their jobs, or a humanoid robot in your home that could not only be able to surveil your entire building but also possibly be stronger and faster than you.
