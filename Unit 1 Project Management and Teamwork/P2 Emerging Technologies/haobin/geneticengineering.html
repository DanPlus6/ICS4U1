Genetic Engineering

Genetic Engineering meaning

Genetic engineering is the process that uses laboratory-based technology to change the DNA makeup of an organism. The process involves changing a single base pair, deleting a region of DNA, or adding a new segment of DNA. An example of this is that genetic engineering may involve adding a gene from one species to an organism from a different species to produce a desired product.

Effects on a minority in Canadian society

The use of genetic engineering could be a threat to Indigenous food sovereignty in Canada. Crops affected by genetically modified crops such as canola, corn, or soy can cross contaminate with the traditional farmland where Indigenous people depend on farms, which affects the quality of food and their health.
