Quantum Computing

Quantum Computing meaning

Quantum computing is a type of computing that uses quantum physics to process information. Instead of using the traditional 1 or 0, it uses qubits, which exist as a combination of 0 and 1 through superposition. Qubits can also be connected through entanglement, which allows quantum computing to solve certain complex problems that normal computers struggle with.

Effects on most of Canadian society

In Canadian society, the use of quantum computing could affect many areas, such as healthcare, cybersecurity, transportation, or research. The National Quantum Strategy is focusing on developing quantum technologies and building Canada's leadership in quantum science. It could allow researchers to develop new medicines and materials for the areas mentioned before.
