Bioinformatics

Bioinformatics meaning

Bioinformatics is a field that is related to genetics and genomics; it's a scientific subdiscipline that involves using computer technology to collect, store, analyze, and changes biological data and info, such as DNA and amino acid sequences, or used for annotation about these sequences. Scientists and clinicians use the databases to organize and index biological information to increase our understanding of health and disease and also healthcare.

Effects on a developing society

Bioinformatics can affect rising societies in a positive way, as bioinformatics could help fight diseases like HIV, AIDS, or common diseases. But there's one problem: bioinformatics can’t be used for every society, as some societies have poor internet, shortages of specialists, and basically don’t have enough money to deploy this technology. This issue is especially real in low-income countries across Africa.
