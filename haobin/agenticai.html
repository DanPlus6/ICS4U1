Agentic Ai

Agentic Ai meaning

Agentic AI is a type of AI system that can accomplish a goal with a limited amount of supervision. It consists of AI agents and machine learning models that have the ability to mimic human decision-making to solve problems in real time. It's a system that uses multiple agents; each of the agents is requested to perform a specific subtask required to reach the goal, and it's coordinated through AI orchestration.

Effects on the world economy

In the real-world economy, this affects it, as agentic AI can be used as a productivity tool for an autonomous workforce. Agentic AI can execute multiple tasks at once without the constant mistakes that humans make. The AI can be used to compress labour costs and make decisions. But the problem with AI is that it can raise trust concerns. That is why there are global standards so that systems can be trusted and used safely across the globe and industries to grow their economy.
